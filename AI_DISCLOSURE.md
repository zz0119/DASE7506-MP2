# AI assistance and personal contribution

The student formed the research question, overall direction and evaluation goals
by connecting ideas from Lectures 4-7. Within that framework, OpenAI Codex helped
extend the direction into a candidate-group scoring model, feature schemes,
training objectives and experimental procedures.

Codex assistance covered technical exploration, code writing, training and
evaluation, portable inference export, tests, result organization and report
drafting. The student's contribution concerns course-informed framing, research
direction, review of experimental interpretation and final choices. The student
remains responsible for understanding and checking the submitted work.

The supplied data and official evaluation tools come from
xudongwu-0/DASE7506-MP2. Their attribution, data license and notice are retained
in code/data/LICENSE.txt and code/data/NOTICE.txt.

All learned quantities are fitted on supplied training data. Validation is used
for development and selection. Test references are reserved for independent
scoring after code and assets are frozen. No external training data, additional
pretrained weights, generation/API calls at evaluation, answer lookup tables,
question-ID features or cached prediction assets are used by the selector.

The report tables use experiments executed during this development cycle and
the official frozen evaluation. Scientific Agent Skills provided procedural
support for evidence appraisal and reporting; the report cites its verified
paper metadata separately from benchmark evidence.

The final English PDF is report/MP2_Report.pdf. It states the assistance scope,
method limitations and resource-measurement limits explicitly.
